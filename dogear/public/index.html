ONE-TIME SETUP. MANY HAPPY RETURNS.

Meet your new bookmark.

Save the passage in view with Safari on your Mac or iPhone. Select a sentence first for an even more precise place.

  1. 01

    Show your Favorites bar

    In Safari’s menu bar, choose View → Show Favorites Bar.

  2. 02

    Drag this button onto the bar

    Save my place

    Drag to the Favorites bar above. Clicking here opens a sample.

  3. 03

    Read. Pause. Click.

    On an article, click Save my place in your Favorites bar. Open Dogear and choose Resume reading to return to that passage.

Already see Save my place in your iPhone Favorites from your Mac? You’re ready to use it. Otherwise, set it up below.

  1. 01

    Add a bookmark in Safari

    On this page, tap Share → Add Bookmark. Name it Save my place, choose Favorites, then tap Save.

  2. 02

    Give the bookmark its code

    Open Safari’s Bookmarks → Favorites. Touch and hold Save my place, choose Edit, and replace the Address with the copied code. Tap Done.

  3. 03

    Save your place on any article

    While reading, open Bookmarks → Favorites → Save my place. Your saved place opens in Dogear in the same tab. Tap Safari’s Back button to keep reading, or choose Resume reading later.
